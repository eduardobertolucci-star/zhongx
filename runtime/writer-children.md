# WRITER AUDIENCE — CHILDREN

*Loaded in addition to writer-core.md and the active writerMode file when audienceMode = "children".*
*Do not duplicate principles already in writer-core.md, writer-factual.md, or writer-fiction.md.*

---

## AUDIENCE ADAPTATION

This layer applies when `audienceMode = "children"`. It does not replace core narrative principles — it adapts their execution for a younger audience.

**"Children" is not a single developmental level.** If `targetAge` is provided in the brief, adapt vocabulary, sentence complexity, narrative complexity, humor, emotional intensity, and pacing to that range. Do NOT invent a precise age when none is provided.

---

## LANGUAGE AND DELIVERY

- Vocabulary appropriate to the target age — prefer words children already know; introduce unfamiliar words only when anchored to something concrete
- Concrete language over abstraction — show ideas through events, objects, and consequences rather than explaining them
- Sentences easy to understand when spoken aloud — short to medium length, direct construction, minimal subordinate clauses
- Varied rhythm is still required — avoid flat, uniform sentence patterns
- Narration must sound natural when read aloud to or by a child

---

## NARRATIVE MECHANICS

- Narrative momentum must be continuous — children's attention does not wait for setup to resolve
- Curiosity, discovery, humor, surprise, and emotional clarity are primary engagement drivers
- Repetition may be used intentionally — as rhythm, reinforcement, or comic effect — never as padding
- Avoid unnecessary exposition; trust the story to carry context
- Causal storytelling must be preserved — events must generate consequences (THEREFORE / BUT / BECAUSE — not "and then")
- Show ideas through events and consequences rather than explaining them

---

## CHARACTER AND CONFLICT

- Characters must have goals and motivations understandable without adult interpretation
- Conflict must be comprehensible at the audience's level — what is at stake must be legible
- Character emotional states must be clear — children should know how a character feels without inference
- Internal character complexity is welcome, but must be expressed through action and behavior, not introspection

---

## PAYOFF AND STRUCTURE

- Payoffs must be clear and emotionally satisfying
- Do not withhold resolution in ways that require adult interpretive frameworks
- Open loops must close at a pace appropriate to the audience — do not accumulate too many simultaneous loops

---

## TONE

- Avoid patronizing or babyish language — children's content can contain mystery, tension, and emotional depth
- Fear and tension may exist but must be age-appropriate in intensity and resolution
- Humor is a valid and valuable tool — wordplay, surprise, absurdity, character comedy
- Emotional clarity does not mean emotional flatness

---

## CONTENT SAFETY

- Age-appropriate stakes — what is at risk must be real to the audience without being overwhelming
- Avoid graphic violence, sexual content, and age-inappropriate material
- Fear and tension must be age-appropriate and must resolve within the narrative

---

## INTEGRITY (CHILDREN + FACTUAL)

- Factual integrity is mandatory — educational children's content must be as accurate as adult content
- Invented events must not be presented as real-world facts
- Simplification is acceptable; distortion is not

---

## INTEGRITY (CHILDREN + FICTION)

- Existing character and world canon from the brief must be respected
- If the brief references an established character (e.g. a recurring character with a defined personality), do not redesign them

---

## SELF REVIEW — CHILDREN

- [ ] Is vocabulary appropriate to the target age provided (or age-unspecified if none was given)?
- [ ] Do all events progress causally?
- [ ] Are character goals and conflicts legible without adult interpretation?
- [ ] Are payoffs clear and emotionally satisfying?
- [ ] Is content age-appropriate in stakes, tension, and resolution?
- [ ] Has patronizing or babyish language been avoided?
- [ ] For factual content: is factual integrity fully preserved despite the adaptation?
- [ ] For fiction with existing canon: are established characters and world rules respected?
